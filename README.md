### 🧭 &nbsp;About Me

I'm a full-stack software engineer with **5 years of experience** designing and shipping distributed systems that critical operations depend on: banking compliance platforms, payment systems, and now, at **Oracle**, a brand-new **cloud-native ERP & Point-of-Sale suite**.

I work end to end: **Java** backends with **JEE** and **Spring Boot** (plus WebFlux and Helidon) built on microservices and hexagonal architecture, **React** and **Angular** frontends, and cloud deployments on **AWS** and **Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI)**. My most significant projects were delivered under strict regulatory constraints (KYC, sanctions screening, SSO) for financial institutions and regulators.

I believe an untested feature isn't done: tests, coverage and observability are part of my definition of done.
